# FitCrew / BodyOS V2.0 Release Notes / FitCrew / BodyOS V2.0 发布说明

## 中文

### 发布摘要

FitCrew V2.0 把一只血糖仪、Apple Watch 和飞书群里零散的日常提醒，连接成一个有清晰边界的私人健康教练体验：**FitCrew** 是产品品牌，**BodyOS** 是面向用户的生活方式教练，**Moticlaw** 负责管理与通道编排。它的目标是帮助用户形成今天能完成的小行动；它不是医疗器械，也不做诊断、治疗或用药建议。

本次公开叙事所依据的代码基线是 PR #2 的合并提交 [`3438e02770a04478913dfeeead029d23a55167f5`](https://github.com/flicy/fitcrew-agent/commit/3438e02770a04478913dfeeead029d23a55167f5)。已部署的健康检查端点为 <https://124.156.218.104/healthz>，返回版本为 `v2.0.0-alpha.1`；V2.0 这一产品叙事不把该后端版本表述为已完成的正式商店发布。

### 隐私优先的健康数据路径

- Apple Health 与 Apple Watch 授权是可选的；用户可以选择不连接。
- 在用户授权的健康类别内，Yuwell CGM 数据以及 Apple Health / Apple Watch 数据可走独立设备、当前同意状态和设备绑定的接入路径。
- 原始健康数值与日特征以加密方式处理；用于教练回复的输入被限制为去标识化的日级汇总、质量状态、意图、约束和带页码的私人知识摘录，而不是原始时间序列或聊天原文。
- 用户可以撤回类别授权；撤回后，新的该类别摄取会被拒绝。导出与删除走受授权的接口。

### 飞书中什么能说、什么不能说

群聊和私聊是两种不同的空间。飞书群仅允许策略层给出的固定、低敏的行为提示；不展示原始健康数据、具体健康或非健康行为详情、跨群信息，或私聊中的内容。需要个性化讨论、授权相关对话或带证据的教练建议时，BodyOS 应在私聊中完成，并遵守其最小化数据输入边界。

### 两位用户也要各自独立

V2.0 的双用户路径把用户、设备和每个健康类别的同意独立绑定到 `fitcrew_user_id`。配对码为一次性使用；相同飞书主体的重复邀请保持幂等。设备不能被第二位用户占用，跨用户上传会被拒绝。重新配对设备时，会轮换设备关联并清除上一设备的本地同步游标与对账时间；授权方也可以撤销访问。这些机制的目的不是制造复杂操作，而是让“我的身体数据只服务于我”成为可验证的默认行为。

### 已验证的交付证据

- Python / policy CI 已通过。
- Swift Core CI 已通过。
- iOS Simulator CI 已通过。
- 生产 TLS 已验证；部署健康检查端点可用，并报告 `v2.0.0-alpha.1`。

这些是构建、策略、模拟器和部署连接性的证据，不等同于对个人健康结果的承诺。

### 明确边界与下一步

- TestFlight 外部分发尚未完成；不能据此宣称用户已经可以通过 TestFlight 公开或远程安装。
- 薛程的真机验收尚未完成。
- 本项目不提供医疗诊断，不替代合格医疗专业人员；症状、用药和疾病治疗应咨询专业人士。
- 尚未完成或观察到一个已经过去的 16 天研究；16 天仅可作为未来经同意、合规执行的实验周期，不能作为已获得结果的表述。
- 真实健康数据、身份标识、设备令牌与一次性配对信息不会进入公开发布材料或 Git。

## English

### Release summary

FitCrew V2.0 connects a glucose meter, an Apple Watch, and scattered daily prompts in a Feishu group into a private health-coach experience with clear boundaries. **FitCrew** is the product brand, **BodyOS** is the user-facing lifestyle coach, and **Moticlaw** manages administration and channel orchestration. The goal is a small action a person can do today—not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, or medication advice.

The public narrative is grounded in the PR #2 merge commit [`3438e02770a04478913dfeeead029d23a55167f5`](https://github.com/flicy/fitcrew-agent/commit/3438e02770a04478913dfeeead029d23a55167f5). The deployed health endpoint, <https://124.156.218.104/healthz>, reports `v2.0.0-alpha.1`. This V2.0 product narrative does not represent that backend version as a completed App Store release.

### A privacy-first health-data path

- Apple Health and Apple Watch authorization are optional; people can choose not to connect them.
- For health categories a person authorizes, Yuwell CGM and Apple Health / Apple Watch data can follow an ingestion path with a dedicated device, current consent, and a device binding.
- Raw health values and daily features are handled in encrypted form. Inputs for coach responses are restricted to de-identified daily aggregates, quality state, intent, constraints, and page-cited private knowledge excerpts—not raw time series or chat transcripts.
- A person can withdraw category consent. New ingestion for that category is then rejected; export and deletion use authorized endpoints.

### What belongs in a Feishu group—and what does not

A group and a direct message are distinct spaces. In Feishu groups, policy permits only fixed, low-sensitivity behavior prompts. No raw health data, detailed health or non-health behavior, cross-group information, or DM content is exposed. Personal discussion, authorization-related conversation, and evidence-backed coaching belong in direct messages, where BodyOS follows its minimum-data boundary.

### Independent by default, even for two users

The V2.0 two-user path independently binds a person, device, and each health-category consent to a `fitcrew_user_id`. Pairing codes are one-time use; reinviting the same Feishu subject is idempotent. A device cannot be claimed by a second person, and cross-user uploads are rejected. Pairing a replacement device rotates the device association and clears the prior device's local sync cursor and reconciliation time; an authorized party can also revoke access. The point is not extra ceremony—it is making “my body data serves me” a verifiable default.

### Verified delivery evidence

- Python / policy CI is green.
- Swift Core CI is green.
- iOS Simulator CI is green.
- Production TLS is verified; the deployed health endpoint is available and reports `v2.0.0-alpha.1`.

This is evidence for builds, policy, simulator coverage, and deployment connectivity. It is not a promise of an individual health outcome.

### Explicit boundaries and next steps

- External TestFlight distribution is not complete. Nothing here claims that people can already install through public or remote TestFlight distribution.
- Xue Cheng's physical-device acceptance is not complete.
- This project does not provide medical diagnosis and does not replace qualified clinicians; symptoms, medication, and disease treatment belong with appropriate professionals.
- No 16-day study has elapsed or been observed. Sixteen days may only describe a future, consented, compliant experiment window—not a completed result.
- Real health data, identities, device tokens, and one-time pairing artifacts are not included in public release materials or Git.

# FitCrew V2：把身体数据、感受与科学知识接进 BodyOS / FitCrew V2: Body data, perception, and scientific knowledge in BodyOS

## 中文

### FitCrew 与 BodyOS 是什么

**FitCrew · AI 健身管理专家。BodyOS 是用户在飞书中接触到的私人生活方式教练。**

FitCrew V2 不只是“接入更多数据”。它把 Apple Watch、鱼跃血糖仪、用户当下的身体感受和科学知识放进同一段持续对话，让 BodyOS 帮助用户理解可能的生活方式关系，并把复杂信息转化为今天能执行的小行动。

### V2 新增的四项能力

1. **Apple Watch 与鱼跃血糖数据**：经用户授权，iPhone HealthKit Bridge 读取已经进入 Apple Health 的 Apple Watch 与鱼跃 Anytime 5 Pro 数据。
2. **食物 × 血糖 × 身体感知**：在 BodyOS 私聊中，把今天吃了什么、血糖如何变化和主观感受放在一起讨论，获得生活方式层面的解释与行动建议。
3. **群聊通用健康互动**：在群聊中回答通用饮食、训练、睡眠与控糖知识问题，也参与打卡和日常健康互动。
4. **三本科学书籍指导**：私人知识库收录《控糖革命》《百岁人生行动手册》《睡眠优化完全指南：科学与实践》，在系统可取得来源时用于辅助回答。

### 群聊与私聊怎么分工

- **群聊**用于通用知识、共同打卡和健康互动。群聊不会读取个人健康数据、私聊内容或私人知识库，也不会公开原始健康数值。
- **BodyOS 私聊**用于对应用户的个人分析。只有用户主动授权后，BodyOS 才会在最小必要范围内使用其数据，讨论食物、血糖、睡眠、训练与身体感知。

### 数据、隐私与医疗边界

- Apple Health 与 Apple 设备不是使用 FitCrew 的前提；健康授权始终可选、可撤回。
- 用户、设备和健康类别同意分别绑定；跨用户上传与调用会被拒绝。
- 原始健康字段加密保存；模型只接收完成回答所需的聚合特征、意图与知识摘录。
- BodyOS 提供生活方式指导，不提供疾病诊断、治疗或用药建议，也不能替代医生。

- Python / policy、Swift Core 与 iOS Simulator CI 已通过。
- 生产严格 HTTPS 健康检查已验证；<https://124.156.218.104/healthz> 当前报告后端版本 `v2.0.0-alpha.1`。
- 受控邀请、一次性配对、按用户隔离、授权撤回以及群聊隐私边界已进入代码基线。

这些证据验证的是构建、策略、隔离与部署连接性，不代表任何个人健康结果。

### 尚未完成

## English

### What FitCrew and BodyOS are

**FitCrew is the AI fitness management expert. BodyOS is the private lifestyle coach people meet in Feishu.**

FitCrew V2 is not simply “more data integrations.” It brings Apple Watch, Yuwell glucose-monitor data, a person's in-the-moment body perception, and scientific knowledge into one continuous conversation. BodyOS helps explore possible lifestyle relationships and turn complicated information into a small action for today.

### Four new V2 capabilities

1. **Apple Watch and Yuwell glucose data:** with permission, the iPhone HealthKit Bridge reads Apple Watch and Yuwell Anytime 5 Pro data that has reached Apple Health.
2. **Food × glucose × body perception:** in a BodyOS DM, a person can discuss what they ate, how glucose changed, and how they felt, then receive a lifestyle explanation and practical next step.
3. **General health interaction in groups:** group chat can answer general questions about food, training, sleep, and glucose management, as well as support check-ins and everyday health interaction.
4. **Guidance from three scientific books:** the private knowledge base includes *《控糖革命》*, *《百岁人生行动手册》*, and *《睡眠优化完全指南：科学与实践》* and uses available source material to support answers.

### How groups and DMs divide the work

- **Groups** are for general knowledge, shared check-ins, and health interaction. A group never reads personal health data, DMs, or private knowledge, and it never exposes raw health values.
- **BodyOS DMs** are for the corresponding person's analysis. Only after explicit permission does BodyOS use the minimum necessary data to discuss food, glucose, sleep, training, and body perception.

### Data, privacy, and medical boundaries

- Apple Health and Apple hardware are not prerequisites for FitCrew. Health permission is always optional and revocable.
- People, devices, and health-category consent bind separately; cross-user ingestion and access are rejected.
- Raw health fields are encrypted at rest, and a model receives only the aggregates, intent, and knowledge excerpts needed for the answer.
- BodyOS provides lifestyle guidance, not disease diagnosis, treatment, or medication advice, and it does not replace a clinician.

### Verified engineering state

- Python / policy, Swift Core, and iOS Simulator CI are green.
- Strict production HTTPS health checks are verified. <https://124.156.218.104/healthz> currently reports backend version `v2.0.0-alpha.1`.
- Controlled invitation, one-time pairing, per-user isolation, consent withdrawal, and the group privacy boundary are in the code baseline.

This verifies builds, policy, isolation, and deployment connectivity—not an individual health outcome.

### Not complete

- External TestFlight distribution is not complete.
- Full physical-device acceptance for an invited tester is not complete.
- The real-world 16-day study is not complete, and there is no study conclusion to publish yet.
- Real health data, identities, device tokens, pairing artifacts, and private book text are not included in the public Release or Git.
